AI made us faster at shipping and slower at recovering
Lead time to change fell by half. Time to recover doubled. It's the same story.

Over two quarters our lead time to change dropped by more than half. In the same stretch, the time it took us to recover from an incident doubled. I don't think that's a coincidence, and I don't think the answer is to slow down.

The numbers

Over the same weeks, the time it took to recover from an incident went from about three hours to more than seven.

Why they moved together

The tools write faster than we read. Developers who use AI assistants accept code they don't fully understand, and that's fine right up until it breaks.

Every major outage last year traced back to AI-written code. When something fails at 2 a.m., nobody on call wrote the change in their head first.

Teams spend 40% of incident time just re-reading the change.
