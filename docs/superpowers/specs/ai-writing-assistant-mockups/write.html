Over two quarters our lead time to change dropped by more than half. In the same stretch, the time it took us to recover from an incident doubled. I don't think that's a coincidence, and I don't think the answer is to slow down.

The numbers

Suggested · section 02AcceptReject

Here's what the last two quarters looked like for one team, on the same services, with no reorg in between. Lead time to change fell from just over six days to two and a half. Over the same weeks, the time it took to recover from an incident went from about three hours to more than seven.

The lines cross in late May, a few weeks after we started using AI coding tools across the team. I'm not going to claim the tools caused both trends on their own. But the timing is hard to ignore, and so is the shape: every week we got faster at shipping, we got slower at fixing what we shipped.
